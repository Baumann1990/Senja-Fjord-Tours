The Fisherman's Haul

The waters around Senja have fed the communities of this coast for thousands of years. Cod, saithe, haddock and coalfish move through these fjords in the shadow of the mountains, following the currents that have driven the fishing economy of northern Norway since long before anyone kept records. On this trip, you go after them the old-fashioned way.

We leave Finnsnes harbour and head out to the fishing grounds that your guide knows from years on this water. When you're in position, your guide sets you up with a handline — the traditional tool of coastal Norwegian fishing — and shows you the technique. There is nothing complicated about it. You lower the line, you wait, you feel. When the line goes heavy, you haul it in yourself.

Once the catch is on board, your guide teaches you how to gut and fillet the fish. These are skills that were once as ordinary along this coast as tying your shoes — passed down through families, taught on boats like this one. You'll leave with the fillets and, if you want them, the words for what you just did in Norwegian.

Throughout the six hours the scenery is always there: Senja's peaks above, the fjord stretching away in every direction, the silence that only open water can provide. The fishing and the landscape are not separate things on this tour — they are the same thing.

Seasonal note: The main commercial fishing season in northern Norway runs through the winter months, when cod migrate south in enormous numbers. This tour operates year-round as a tourist experience — we use the same grounds, the same methods, and the same boat. Catches are real but cannot be guaranteed. Your guide will find the best spots available on the day.
